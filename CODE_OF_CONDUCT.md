# Code of conduct

This is a small project and we want it to stay pleasant to take part in.

- Be kind, and assume the other person is trying to help.
- Criticise the work, not the person. Say what went wrong and what you expected.
- No harassment, insults, or posting other people's private details.
- Keep to the topic in issues and Discussions. Advertising your own product is fine only when it answers the question asked.

Anyone who breaks these may have comments hidden or be blocked. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or use the private reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
