The app's top bar is Chat, Knowledge, Agents, Usage and Settings; the Agents tab holds the timeline, map, history and the rest of that group as a row of pages. Chat's own pages (Inbox, Files, Documents and Mail) are links at the foot of its conversation list. Settings gains a Buying page once the buy module is added. In a conversation you can open a [side chat](guide.md#the-app) to ask about part of an answer, and the tour the app offers on a new Hive shows how. While an agent works and the message box is empty, Send turns into Stop, which ends the run at once. The [guide](guide.md#the-app) walks through each.
