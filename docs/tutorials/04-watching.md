# 4. Keep an eye on the work and your usage

## The quick check

The top bar answers the two usual questions on every page: how many agents are working, and how much of the week is used. The number on **Chat** says how many things need you.

If you only do one thing, look at the Chat home page: **Waiting for your answer**, **New for you**, **Stopped** and **Working now**.

## See what agents are doing

Click **Agents**. **Now** shows who is working. For the full list, open **All agents** in the row under the bar: **Running** and **Failed** narrow it, and **Filters** and **Columns** change what you see.

![The Agents page](https://hivelab.ca/img/guide/agents.png)

Each row shows what the agent was asked, what it last said and what it's doing now. Click an agent's name for the details, including its recent tool calls.

A *quiet* agent isn't a problem. It's usually waiting for you, or for a job. Worry about one when it says *stuck*, or when it's been quiet on something you expected to move. Ask it in the conversation: "Where are you on this?"

## Check your usage

Click **Usage**.

![The Usage page](https://hivelab.ca/img/guide/usage.png)

The first sentence tells you how much is left, when it comes back and when you'd run out at this pace. Dollar figures are at API list prices. On a subscription they measure work, and aren't a bill.

**If it's going too fast:**

1. Set **Agents at once** in Settings, or lower it. 1 or 2 is fine for most people.
2. Look at the conversations that used the most, and tell the agent to stop if the job isn't worth it.
3. Lower the **Usage line**, so new agents stop sooner and keep some of the week for your own use.
4. Make sure **Keep work moving** is off in Settings, unless you want it.

**If you need more room:** add another Claude subscription in Settings, under **Accounts**. New agents go to the one with the most left.

## Go through everything that happened

- **Inbox**, at the foot of the Chat list: everything addressed to you.
- **Agents > Timeline:** which conversations ran when.
- **Agents > Map:** who's talking to whom, with **Replay**.
- **Agents > History:** every event, newest first.
- **Usage > This machine:** how busy the computer is.
