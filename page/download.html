Download Hive

One file, double-click, and you are in the Hive. The terminal install line still works too.

Windows 10/11 — Hive-Setup.exe macOS 13+ — Hive.dmg Linux — Hive.deb

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That is expected: the app is not signed yet. Press More info, then Run anyway. Windows may ask permission once, and may need one restart; open Hive again afterwards and it carries on.

Mac says "Hive" Not Opened
  1. Press Done (not Move to Trash).
  2. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security. It says "Hive" was blocked to protect your Mac. Press Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  3. Hive opens. The Mac asks this once only.
Linux

Double-click the .deb (or run sudo apt install ./Hive-linux-amd64.deb), then open Hive from your applications.
